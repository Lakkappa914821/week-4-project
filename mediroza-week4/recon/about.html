About Mediroza

Mediroza General Hospital has served the Johannesburg community since 2003. What began as a small day clinic has grown into a 250 bed general hospital with more than 40 specialists across cardiology, emergency care, pediatrics, radiology and pathology.

Our mission is simple. We give every patient careful, respectful treatment backed by modern equipment and a team that works around the clock.

Our values

Patient comfort, clinical excellence and honesty in everything we do. We invest in our people and in the technology that helps them deliver safe care.

Accreditation

Our diagnostics laboratory is SANAS accredited and our wards are inspected regularly to keep national standards.
